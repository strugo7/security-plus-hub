# Lesson: Asset Management
**Module:** 4.2

## Introduction
A critical part of information security is the ability to manage the organization's hardware and software. In this lesson, we will learn about the procurement process, asset tracking, media sanitization (cleaning), and physical destruction.

## 1. The Procurement Process
Every organization has a formal process for acquiring goods and services from third parties. This is part of the purchasing process, and it is often a very formal procedure which requires sign-off and approval by a number of different parts of your organization.

## 2. Asset Tracking
The moment a physical product (such as a laptop) is received in the organization, it is entered into a central asset tracking system. The goal is to manage the asset throughout its entire lifecycle.
*What does the system include?*
• *Ownership Assignment:* The first step is to define who holds the asset. If an employee receives a laptop, the computer is registered under their name. If an update or repair is needed, IT knows exactly who to contact.
• *Categorization and Taxes (Hardware vs. Software):* The system differentiates between hardware and software, which has economic significance:
    ◦ *Hardware:* Considered a Capital Expenditure. It has Depreciation over time, which affects taxes.
    ◦ *Software:* Usually does not accumulate depreciation, and is therefore considered an Operating Expense. Its taxation is different.
• *Integration with Help Desk:* The tracking system assists the support team. When a trouble ticket is opened, the technician can immediately see the device model and its technical specifications, knowing exactly what they are dealing with.
• *Component Breakdown:* The system does not view a "computer" as a black box, but rather knows how to detail what is inside it – CPU, memory, hard drives, and peripherals.
• *Asset Tags:* It is customary to attach a physical sticker with a barcode or serial number to the device. This allows for quick identification upon scanning and also serves as a security measure and deterrent against theft.

## 3. Media Sanitization & Destruction
When an asset finishes its role or is transferred to another employee, we must handle the sensitive data on the hard drive.
*Option A: Reuse*
If the computer is transferred to another employee within the organization, we will want to delete the data but keep the drive intact.
• *Secure Delete/Wipe:* Using software that deletes the data so that it cannot be recovered, but leaves the hardware usable.
